# Privacy Policy – Final Approach

_Last updated: 11 November 2026_

Final Approach ("the game") is a cartoon-style air traffic game for Android, made by HexMaster ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens with your information when you play.

## In short

**We do not collect, store, share or sell your personal information.** The game works entirely on your device.

## What we collect

Nothing. Specifically, the game:

- does not ask you to create an account or sign in;
- does not request any Android permissions (no location, contacts, camera, microphone, storage or network access);
- does not connect to the internet or to any server of ours;
- does not include analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking software;
- does not collect your device identifiers, advertising ID or location.

## Information on your device

The game may keep gameplay data such as settings or scores on your device so they are there next time you play. This data stays on your device, is not sent anywhere, and is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its storage. Automatic Android backups are turned off for the game.

## Google Play

The game is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect information when you download or update the game, as described in the [Google Play Terms of Service](https://play.google.com/about/play-terms/) and [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). We do not control this and receive no personal information from it. If the game is shared through Play's testing tracks, testers' email addresses are held by Google, not by us.

## Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Because we collect no personal information, we do not knowingly collect information from children.

## Third parties

The game does not share data with third parties, because it has no data to share and makes no network connections.

## Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy (for example, adding ads or online features), we will update this policy and the "Last updated" date before releasing that version. The current version is always available in this repository at `docs/privacy-policy.md`.

## Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue at <https://github.com/hexmasternl/final-approach/issues>.
