# Privacy Policy – Final Approach

_Last updated: 16 November 2026_

Final Approach ("the game") is a cartoon-style air traffic game for Android, made by HexMaster ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens with your information when you play. The current version is always at <https://github.com/hexmasternl/final-approach/blob/main/docs/privacy-policy.md>.

## In short

- **The game itself collects no personal information.** There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics and no tracking of ours.
- **The game shows ads from Google AdMob.** Every player is treated as a child, so ads are child-directed, never personalised and limited to content suitable for general audiences. The game does not use your advertising ID.
- Your scores, progress and settings stay on your device.

## Ads and the consent message

The game shows ads from Google AdMob in two places: a full-screen ad before a level starts (and when you restart it), and a banner below the play field during play.

Before any ad is requested, Google may show a consent message. The game plays as normal, without ads, when the message is not accepted or cannot load, for example when your device is offline.

Every ad request is treated as coming from a child: it is tagged as child-directed and as coming from a user under the age of consent, ads are limited to the "G" content rating, and ads are never personalised. The game does not ask your age.

## What Google may collect

To serve and measure ads, and to keep them safe, Google may collect information from your device through its ads software, such as your IP address, device and app details (for example device model, operating system version and the app's name and version) and how you interact with ads. Because ads here are child-directed, Google does not use this information to personalise ads and does not use your advertising ID. Google handles this information under its own privacy policy. You can read how Google uses data from apps that use its services at <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>, and Google's privacy policy at <https://policies.google.com/privacy>.

We do not receive this information, and we do not combine it with anything else.

## Internet and permissions

The game uses the internet only to load ads and the consent message. It asks for no other permissions: no location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage. It does not read or use your advertising ID.

## Information on your device

The game keeps gameplay data on your device so it is there next time you play: your best score per level, your progress (how many aircraft you have landed and points you have earned per level, and which levels are open), whether you finished the tutorial, and your settings. This data is not sent anywhere. Automatic Android backups are turned off for the game, and the data is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its storage.

## Google Play

The game is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect information when you download or update the game, as described in the [Google Play Terms of Service](https://play.google.com/about/play-terms/) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). We do not control this and receive no personal information from it. If the game is shared through Play's testing tracks, testers' email addresses are held by Google, not by us.

## Children

The game is meant for all ages, including children. The game itself collects no personal information from anyone. Every ad request is treated as coming from a child, and ads are child-directed, non-personalised and limited to content suitable for general audiences. If you are a parent or guardian and have questions, please get in touch (see below).

## Third parties

The only third party is Google, through Google AdMob and its consent message, as described above. We share no data with anyone else.

## Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example by adding another software library or online feature, we will update this policy and the "Last updated" date before releasing that version. The current version is always at <https://github.com/hexmasternl/final-approach/blob/main/docs/privacy-policy.md>.
