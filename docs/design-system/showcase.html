FinalApproach

The design system for a top-down air traffic game that looks like a box of toys tipped onto a lawn.

FoundationsPrinciples

1Outline everythingEvery drawn shape carries a thick outline stroke with round joins. No hairlines, no shape without an edge.
2Flat and brightFills are single flat colours. No gradients or textures. Only things that fly get a soft shadow.
3Toy scaleSizes are exaggerated, not realistic. A jumbo is nearly twice a light prop.
4Silhouette before colourTypes, zones and states are told apart by shape first. Colour confirms.
5The field stays clearUI lives in small outlined chips at the edges. Nothing covers a landing zone.

FoundationsColour

71 colour tokens in three themes: Day, Night and Dawn. The world is lawn, asphalt and white aircraft; the UI lives in the sky; sun yellow is the signature. Swatches follow the theme switch; values list Day first.
