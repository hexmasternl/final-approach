A round landing spot for helicopters.

**You provide:** position and a `state`.

112px across at 1x: `asphalt-500` disc with a `line-3` `outline`, a 6px `marking-warn` ring, and a `marking` H with 9px rounded strokes. States work exactly as on Runway: `ok` is a solid pulsing `zone-ok` halo, `no` a dotted `zone-no` halo (an airplane is selected and its path ends here).

The landing rule is distance-based: the path end must fall within the pad radius. Keep the visual radius and the rule radius equal.
