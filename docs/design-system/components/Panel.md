A paper-light container for grouped content in menus.

**You provide:** a title and the content.

`surface` fill, `line-3` `outline`, `radius-lg`, `shadow-lip-lg`, `space-5` padding. Title in `title-md`, body in `body`, secondary text in `ink-muted`. Panels stay light in the Night theme so their outline keeps reading.

**Don't** nest panels. Inside a panel, separate groups with `space-4` and use `surface-sunken` wells for anything that needs its own box.
