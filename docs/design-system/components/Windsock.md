A striped windsock on a pole beside the runway.

**You provide:** position. The element `fa-windsock` shows the scenery sprite `windsock`; `fa-scenery` with `name="windsock"` shows the same sprite at a size.

**Construction.** Seen from above, as a standing sprite of height tier L (W5): a `concrete-300` foot, an `outline` pole with a `tin-300` glint, and the sock lying downwind in `tangerine-500` with `foam` stripes and a darker lower half. The sock is outlined at `line-ui-sm`. The windsock is one of the three accents scenery keeps (W9). It casts a hard shadow of the pole and the sock down-right in the shared shadow layer (W1).

As a standing sprite it does not rotate: the sock always lies the same way.

**Motion.** On the field the sock waves once per `dur-sway` with `ease-sway`. With reduced motion it is still.

Today it is ambience. When wind arrives as a mechanic (a listed future idea), the sock becomes an instrument and this guideline changes with it.

**Don't** use `tangerine-500` on any other scenery, and don't place a windsock on a runway's fringe or approach end.

**Compose.** The app draws its windsock from the version 1 scenery sprites until the app's world is ported.
