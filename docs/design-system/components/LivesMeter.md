Remaining lives as a row of hearts.

**You provide:** lives left and lives total (three by default), and an accessible label such as "2 of 3 lives left".

Hearts are the `heart` icon at 30px, `cherry` with a 3.5px `outline` stroke. A lost life stays in place as a smaller (78%) heart in `surface-sunken`, so the count reads by shape and size as well as colour. On losing a life, shake the chip 6px sideways twice in `dur-pop`.

On the hardest difficulty a collision ends the game: show a single heart.
