FoundationsMotion

Bouncy and short. Things arrive with one overshoot, controls snap, and the world never stops moving a little.

Easing

TokenValueFeelUse
ease-bouncecubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1)Anything arriving: pop-ins, dialog drop, selection ring. Overshoots once.
ease-snapcubic-bezier(0.2, 0.9, 0.3, 1)State changes of controls: press, toggle, segment slide.
ease-out-softcubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)Things leaving or fading: score popups rising, poof clearing.
ease-swaycubic-bezier(0.45, 0, 0.55, 1)Ambient back-and-forth: windsock, idle bob.
