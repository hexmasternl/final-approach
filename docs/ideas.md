# Ideas – Final Approach

Feature ideas and potential unique selling points. Builds on the core loop: draw paths and sequence aircraft of different speeds. Nothing here is committed; promote ideas to `game-design.md` and `roadmap.md` when chosen.

## Strongest USPs (pick 2–3 as the identity)
1. **Personality-driven aircraft.** Each plane and pilot has a quirk, shown through the callsign chip and cartoon animation: a nervous student pilot wobbles, a cocky jet hot-dogs, a sleepy cargo hauler is slow to respond.
2. **Draw-to-land with an ink budget.** Paths cost ink; longer or sharper paths use more. Prevents huge spirals, makes routing a puzzle, and is a natural meta-upgrade currency.
3. **Daily Tower shift.** One seeded airport and spawn schedule per day, shared by everyone, with friends leaderboards. The deterministic simulation makes this cheap.
4. **Replay and ghost controller.** Record inputs and replay them as a short shareable clip, or race a friend's ghost on the same seed.
5. **Hand-crafted real-world airports.** Stylised famous layouts (crossing runways, a helipad on a skyscraper) as themed packs.

## Gameplay depth
- **Wind and weather.** Crosswinds drift aircraft, fog hides unselected ones, storm cells are no-go zones.
- **Emergency aircraft.** Low fuel (countdown ring) and priority medical helicopters force re-sequencing.
- **Holding patterns.** Draw a loop to hold a plane. Helicopters loop tightly; jumbos need big loops.
- **Wake turbulence.** A jumbo leaves a danger zone behind it, making separation visible.
- **Ground ops.** Planes taxi off after landing, blocking the runway a little longer; add take-offs that merge into traffic.
- **Special aircraft.** Seaplane (lands on water), blimp (slow obstacle), stunt plane (fixed route).
- **Combos.** Back-to-back or near-simultaneous landings build a "smooth sequencing" multiplier.

## Feel and accessibility
- **Tower radio chatter.** Short, funny voice or text lines in the friendly-controller tone.
- **One-thumb, no-text play.** Silhouettes and shapes over colour alone; market as colour-blind friendly.
- **Zen mode.** No fail state, calm soundtrack.
- **Night levels.** Runway lights and landing beams.

## Meta and retention
- **Airport upgrades.** Spend landing coins on a longer runway, a second helipad, or a radar that previews arrivals.
- **Unlockable liveries and callsign styles.** Cosmetic only.
- **Career mode.** Short story with a quirky tower crew; milestone levels as chapters.
- **Co-op.** Two players on one tablet, or two phones controlling one airspace.

## Suggested first bundle
Personality quirks, ink budget, emergency aircraft, daily seeded shift, replay clips. Everything else follows once the core loop is fun.
